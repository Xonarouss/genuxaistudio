Welcome to Genux AI Studio!

Genux AI is a high-performance Windows desktop workstation for local AI video 
character recasting, motion transfer, and autonomous cinematography powered by 
NVIDIA TensorRT FP8 and Gemma 4.

QUICK START:
1. Launch Genux AI from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.
2. If prompted on first launch, choose whether to opt in to anonymous GPU performance telemetry.
3. Check Settings -> Engine & Models to verify your model paths and GPU acceleration.
4. Import your video or search trending viral clips, upload a character portrait, and click Recast!

REQUIREMENTS:
- Windows 10 / 11 64-bit
- NVIDIA RTX GPU (12GB+ VRAM recommended, e.g. RTX 3060, 4070, 4080, 5080)
- NVIDIA Drivers 550.0+ with CUDA 12.8+ support

SUPPORT & DOCUMENTATION:
Visit https://docs.genuxai.io for step-by-step guides, troubleshooting, and model installation tips.
